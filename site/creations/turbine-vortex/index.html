About the living artwork

Measured RPM turns three distinct blade trails, spaced 120 degrees apart, together with the polygonal walls. The three-blade motion uses the measured RPM; a separate slow artistic roll turns the whole scene. The rotor itself is hidden; this is an artistic wake, not a fluid simulation. Wind controls travel and ripples, power controls colour and streak density while its deviation from a 30-second mean controls brightness, and wind-vane angle steers the tunnel. These are artistic mappings, not a physical simulation. History builds from live readings during this visit; startup geometry uses the first reading throughout.

One combined request every two seconds. Observation timestamps come from the data, displayed in your local timezone. The visible-history span shows the oldest and newest observations still represented. Freshness uses API-relative signal ages and elapsed time since observations advance, not differences from the computer clock. If any signal is invalid, unavailable or stops advancing for over 60 seconds, the artwork rests with labelled retained readings. No simulated readings replace live data.

Immerse fills the screen. Its controls fade after six seconds of inactivity; move, tap or use the keyboard to bring them back. Connection warnings remain visible. Pause and reduced-motion support are available throughout.
